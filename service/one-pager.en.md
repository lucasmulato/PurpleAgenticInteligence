# AI Agent Action Audit

**Problem.** AI agents suggest and execute actions. A prompt injection or an
over-broad permission can cause damage before anyone notices.

**Approach.** Five fixed-scope steps: action inventory, risk map, executable
policy, adversarial tests (including injection through external content) and a
report in Portuguese. See [AUDITORIA.md](AUDITORIA.md).

**Demo.** The deterministic SAFE-AI gateway decides `PERMIT`, `REJECT` or
`ASK_HUMAN` and records everything at `/audit`. Ninety-second script:
[DEMO-SCRIPT.md](../docs/DEMO-SCRIPT.md).

**Deliverables.** Report, policy file in SAFE-AI format, test evidence and an
explicit list of known limits.

**Offer.** Fixed scope, no mandatory subscription and no platform to buy. Price
and timing are agreed in the scope document per engagement — we publish no
number without Lucas's decision.

We do not guarantee compliance with LGPD or with the AI bill. The service
delivers evidence and explicit limits.
